One button captures everything.

The raised plus follows you through the whole app. Whatever lands in your hands, it already knows where it goes.

  • Quick-add to any section. A cost, a to-do, an event, a grocery item, a listing URL. Two taps from anywhere.
  • Photos route themselves. An invoice becomes a cost with the document filed. A receipt attaches to today's shop and reads its own total.
  • Search the whole household. One fuzzy search across every section, straight to the item's editor.
  • Share into it from other apps. Send a listing URL or a PDF to HomeUp and it lands in the right place.

First boot generates the JWT and API keys into your .env, applies every database migration, and brings the whole stack up at localhost:3000.

Your household's data, on your own box.

The entire backend is one compose file: the full Supabase stack, trimmed to what a household actually needs.

  • No cloud account anywhere. Sign in with email and password against your own auth server. Nothing phones home.
  • Keys manage themselves. Secrets are generated on first boot and reused after. Migrations run on every start.
  • Extras are opt-in. Bring a Mapbox token for maps. AI parsing, Google Calendar, and push keys are optional and degrade quietly.

Bring your own agent.

An MCP server is built into the app itself. Issue a personal access token in settings, point any MCP client at your instance, and your assistant can read the budget, add groceries, and book tasks like a member of the household.
